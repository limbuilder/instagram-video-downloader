How to Download Instagram Videos

  1. Step 1 — Copy the video link. Open Instagram, tap the ⋯ menu on the post (or Share on a Reel), and choose Copy Link. On desktop, copy the address from your browser bar.
  2. Step 2 — Paste it above. Drop the link into the box and press Download. We read the file straight from Instagram's servers — nothing to install.
  3. Step 3 — Save to your device. Preview the result, then tap Download to save the MP4 at its original resolution.

Tip: Reels are usually vertical 9:16 — on iPhone the file lands in Files → Downloads, on Android in Gallery or Downloads.

Yes — for Reels and videos the tool offers an audio-only download alongside the video file.
